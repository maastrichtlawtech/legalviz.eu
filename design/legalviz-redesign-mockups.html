Design concepts · July 2026

Redesign mock-ups for LegalViz.EU

Five annotated screens — home, law overview, the article reader, parallel languages, and search — keeping everything the tool already does, but giving it a visual identity rooted in EU law (Official-Journal navy & gold, a proper serif for the law itself) and fixing the main way-finding friction: ambiguous weekday labels, shouting all-caps chapter boxes, and context that hides in the sidebar. Toggle your theme — every mock-up is designed for light and dark.

Data Act

AI overview Generated — verify against the official text

Purpose

Harmonised rules on making data from connected products and related services available to users, data holders, recipients and public bodies; on switching between data processing services; and on safeguards against unlawful third-party access. Art. 1

Key points

  • Connected products must make the data they generate easily, securely and freely accessible to users by default.Art. 3
  • On a user’s request, data holders must share data with third parties on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms.Art. 5 Art. 8
  • Unilaterally imposed unfair contractual terms on data access are not binding.Art. 13
  • Providers must remove obstacles to switching cloud services and guard non-personal data held in the EU against unlawful foreign governmental access.Art. 23 Art. 32

Article reader — the text, with its context beside it

Chapter I · General provisions1

Article 1

Subject matter and scope

1.
This Regulation lays down harmonised rules, inter alia, on:
  1. (a)the making available of product data and related service data to the user of the connected product or related service;
  2. (b)the making available of data by data holders to data recipients;
  3. (c)the making available of data by data holders to public sector bodies, the Commission, the European Central Bank and Union bodies, where there is an exceptional need for those data for the performance of a specific task carried out in the public interest;
  4. (d)facilitating switching between data processing services;
  5. (e)introducing safeguards against unlawful third-party access to non-personal data; and
  6. (f)the development of interoperability standards for data to be accessed, transferred and used.
2.
This Regulation covers personal and non-personal data, including the following types of data, in the following contexts:
  1. (a)Chapter II applies to data, with the exception of content, concerning the performance, use and environment of connected products and related services;
  2. (b)Chapter III applies to any private sector data that is subject to statutory data sharing obligations;
2
data holder Art. 2(13)

A natural or legal person that has the right or obligation, in accordance with this Regulation, to use and make available data, including, where contractually agreed, product data or related service data.

04

Parallel languages — one text, two columns, no drift

The bilingual view is a genuinely distinctive feature, but today it renders as two separate cards with duplicated “SECOND LANGUAGE” labels and independently flowing text. Here both versions live in one grid: a single paragraph-number gutter, rows aligned per paragraph, and the contents rail collapses to a strip to protect the reading measure.

If you only change five things

Roughly ordered by effort — the first column is CSS-and-copy-level work in the existing components; the second column touches layout and data flow.

quick win

Relative dates + resume position

Replace “Monday / Thursday” with “2 days ago” and store the last article per law (it’s already in the URL) — LandingLibrary.jsx.

quick win

Sentence-case TOC with article ranges

Stop uppercasing chapter titles; add “Art. 1–2” ranges and quiet roman numerals. Biggest legibility gain per line of code.

quick win

Status in the law header

Surface “In force · applies from …” pills from the metadata you already fetch; label the AI overview as generated.

bigger move

TOC left, context right

Move contents to the left rail and give recitals / case law / cited-by a tabbed rail next to the article in LawViewer.jsx.

bigger move

One “go to” box

Merge the Article/Recital inputs into a single parser (“23”, “rec 40”) with a g shortcut, and add j/k prev-next keys.

bigger move

Definition popovers

Upgrade the dotted defined-terms to popovers quoting the Art. 2 definition with a link — the parser already extracts definitions.

quick win

Group search results

Split results into “Best match” and the rest using the MiniSearch score you already have, and add a muted “Repealed” pill — the ranking doesn’t change, but it stops reading as broken.

bigger move

Align parallel languages

Render both language versions into one grid keyed by paragraph number instead of two independent cards, and collapse the TOC to a strip while parallel mode is on.
